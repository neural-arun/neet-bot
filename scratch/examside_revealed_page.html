One main scale division of a Vernier calliper is equal to 1 mm and the number of divisions on the Vernier scale is 10 . When both the jaws touch each other, the Vernier scale shifts to the left of zero of the main scale in such a way that 4th  Vernier division coincides with a division of the main scale. If this Vernier calliper measures the length of the wire to be 1 cm , the actual length of the wire is :

A

1.04 cm

B

0.60 cm

C

0.96 cm

D

1.00 cm

Explanation

⇒ There is negative zero error

⇒ Zero error =−(10−4)× L.C. … (i)

From … (i),

Zero error =−(10−4)×0.01=−0.06 cm

⇒ Zero correction =− Zero error

    =−(−0.06)

    =0.06 cm

⇒ Correct reading =R′

R′=R+ Zero correction

     =1+0.06

     =1.06 cm

∴ No option is correct

Each side of a metallic cube of mass 5.580 kg is measured to the 9.0 cm . Keeping the significant figures in view, the density of the material of the cube can be best expressed as X×103 kg m−3 where the value of X is:

A

7.654

B

7.6

C

7.65

D
Correct Answer

7.7

Explanation

The side of the cube is

a=9.0 cm=9.0×10−2 m

The volume of the cube is

V=a3

V=(9.0×10−2)3 m3

V=7.29×10−4 m3

Density is

ρ=massvolume

ρ=5.5807.29×10−4

ρ=7.654×103 kg m−3

Now we apply significant figures.

Mass 5.580 kg has 4 significant figures.

Side 9.0 cm has 2 significant figures.

Since density involves multiplication/division, the final answer should have the least number of significant figures, which is 2.

So,

7.654×103≈7.7×103 kg m−3

Hence,

X=7.7

Correct option: D.
